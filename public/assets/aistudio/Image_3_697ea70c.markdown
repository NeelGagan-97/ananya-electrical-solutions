## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, precision-engineered presence for a Super Grade electrical contracting and solar infrastructure firm. The visual identity bridges heavy-duty transmission engineering with modern clean-energy innovation. It moves intentionally away from generic commercial contractor aesthetics toward an institutional, infrastructure-grade technical aesthetic.

The target audience includes industrial plant directors, infrastructure project managers, commercial enterprise developers, and government utility liaisons who prioritize zero-compromise safety, technical compliance, and long-term grid reliability.

### Design Movement: Modern Technical Engineering
The aesthetic merges high-precision corporate engineering with crisp modern minimalism:
- **Disciplined Precision:** Architectural structure, precise alignment, micro-borders, and high legibility.
- **Tonal Contrast:** Midnight navy and graphite foundations offset by high-luminance electric azure blue and warning-energy amber accents.
- **Technical Rigor:** Crisp stat counters, monospaced metadata callouts, engineered badge treatments, and structured data hierarchy that evoke telemetry dashboards and electrical schematics.

## Colors

The color palette reflects electrical power, safety discipline, and high-voltage precision:

- **Primary (`#0B192C`):** Heavy industrial midnight navy. Conveys authority, grounding, structural permanence, and institutional credibility. Used for primary typography, dark hero segments, navigation headers, and authoritative containers.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`):** Electric azure blue. Represents active current, photovoltaic generation, and modern tech capability. Used for interactive states, key iconography, and dynamic focus points.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`):** Warning & Energy Amber. Represents electrical potential, grid power, critical metrics, and certifications. Used judiciously for Super Grade badges, status alerts, and focal metric counters.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Engineering slate gray. Applied to secondary labels, system metadata, field descriptors, and structural dividers without visual clutter.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#F8FAFC` & `#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity light canvas ensuring maximum readability for industrial specifications, project matrices, and compliance records.

## Typography

The typography pairing creates an architectural balance between structural presence and technical utility:

- **Headlines & Metric Counters (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with firm geometric weight, clean terminals, and disciplined tight tracking. Conveys forward-looking technical prowess and high-capacity engineering capability.
- **Body & Data Readouts (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and systematically engineered for technical documentation, load ratings, technical specifications, and dense contracting terms.
- **Hierarchical Rhythm:** Headlines leverage negative tracking (-0.01em to -0.02em) to maintain visual tension and density. Stat counters stand out with heavy weights to emphasize numerical milestones (e.g., Megawatts commissioned, substations built, KV ratings).
